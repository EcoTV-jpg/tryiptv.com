# AI Visibility Measurement

## Google
Use Search Console's available performance reporting for generative AI features when present on the property.

Also review:
- Web search performance
- landing pages
- queries
- devices/countries
- Discover where applicable
- multimodal search reporting where available

Do not infer AI Overview inclusion from traffic alone.

## Bing
Use AI Performance / AI visibility data:
- citations
- cited pages
- grounding queries
- intents
- topics
- citation share
- trends

## ChatGPT
Use analytics referral data when available.
Segment landing pages and conversions.
Do not treat referral count as “AI rank.”

## KPI hierarchy

### Discovery
- indexed eligible pages
- bot access
- crawl errors
- canonical consistency

### Visibility
- impressions
- cited pages
- AI citations
- grounding queries/topics
- supported generative Search reporting

### Engagement
- clicks
- engaged sessions
- task completion
- conversion

### Business
- qualified trials/leads/sales
- assisted conversions
- revenue where measurable

## Experiments
When testing a change:
- define hypothesis
- choose page cohort
- record baseline
- avoid simultaneous major changes
- choose measurement window
- record confounders
- do not generalize from one URL
