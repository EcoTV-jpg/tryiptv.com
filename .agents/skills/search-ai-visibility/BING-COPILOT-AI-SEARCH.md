# Bing, Copilot, and AI Search

## Discovery foundation
Maintain:
- crawlable pages
- clean canonical signals
- XML sitemaps
- accurate lastmod where used
- internal links
- consistent metadata
- IndexNow for timely changed/added/deleted URLs

IndexNow is a notification protocol; a successful submission is not a guarantee of indexing or ranking.

## AI visibility
Bing Webmaster Tools now provides AI-oriented reporting for supported AI experiences.

Potential metrics include:
- total citations
- cited pages
- grounding queries
- visibility trends
- intents
- topics
- citation share
- comparison with other cited sources

Do not interpret citation count as a conventional rank position.

## Content patterns Bing recommends
For pages intended to be cited:
- clear subject focus
- depth/expertise
- clear headings
- useful tables where appropriate
- FAQ sections only when they help users
- evidence for claims
- accurate/fresh information
- consistent entities across text/images/video

## Duplicate content
Check duplicate/near-duplicate URLs because they can blur preferred-page and intent signals.

## Bing-specific measurement loop
1. Identify cited pages.
2. Identify grounding queries/intents/topics.
3. Compare cited vs indexed-but-uncited pages.
4. Improve clarity, completeness, evidence, and differentiation.
5. Submit meaningful changes through IndexNow.
6. Re-measure citation activity.

Do not create “citation bait” pages with no user value.
