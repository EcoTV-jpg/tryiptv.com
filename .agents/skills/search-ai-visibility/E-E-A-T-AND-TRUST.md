# Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust

Use E-E-A-T as a quality/trust framework, not a magic score.

## Inspect
- factual claims
- business/entity identity
- contact methods
- policies
- pricing consistency
- refund/cancellation terms
- author/reviewer information where topic warrants it
- first-hand screenshots/tests
- methodology
- update history
- citations to primary sources

## Claims
For every strong claim ask:
- Is it true?
- Is it visible elsewhere consistently?
- Is there evidence?
- Does wording overpromise?
- Is the claim time-sensitive?

## Trust problems
Examples:
- contradictory prices
- “instant” in one page vs “5–15 minutes” elsewhere
- fake uptime
- fake reviews
- unsupported “#1” or “best” claims presented as objective fact
- outdated legal/payment statements

Distinguish brand positioning from factual comparative claims.
