# International SEO

## Inspect
- locale URL structure
- language targeting
- country targeting
- hreflang
- canonical interaction
- translated/localized content
- locale switcher links
- automatic redirects
- currency/price consistency

## Hreflang rules
Check:
- valid language/region codes
- reciprocal annotations
- self-reference
- canonical indexable targets
- x-default where appropriate
- no redirect/error URLs

## Localization
Translation alone may be insufficient.
Evaluate:
- terminology
- market-specific examples
- currency
- support/payment availability
- legal claims
- spelling
- cultural fit

## Avoid
- IP-based hard redirects that block crawl/user choice
- mass machine-translated thin pages
- canonicals collapsing intentionally localized equivalents
