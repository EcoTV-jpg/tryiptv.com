## Critical rule
There is no guaranteed method to “rank #1 in AI”.

The system optimizes **eligibility, retrieval quality, citation-worthiness, differentiation, technical clarity, and measurable user/business value**.

## Google 2026 position
Google explicitly treats optimization for generative Search as SEO. Its generative features are rooted in core Search systems, can use RAG and query fan-out, and do not require special AI files/schema.

## Bing 2026 position
Bing Webmaster Tools exposes AI citation/grounding visibility data, allowing a genuine measurement loop instead of guessing about GEO.

## AI crawler principle
Search/retrieval bots and training bots are not necessarily the same. Manage them separately based on current official documentation.
