# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Cord-cutters, sports fans, and international television viewers seeking reliable live TV and on-demand entertainment across personal streaming hardware (Amazon Fire TV Stick, Android TV, Apple TV, iOS, Android, Windows, and Smart TVs) without traditional long-term cable contracts or hardware rental fees.

## Product Purpose
Deliver high-performance, contract-free IPTV streaming with extensive global coverage (25,000+ live channels worldwide, 120,000+ movies and TV shows). Success means frictionless setup and activation (5–15 minutes), zero unexpected rebilling, dependable multi-screen streaming (2 simultaneous connections included by default), and comprehensive self-service device setup guidance.

## Positioning
Prepaid, contract-free IPTV streaming featuring 2 concurrent connections included standard on all tiers, an authentic 24-hour free trial with no credit card required, and strictly no automatic renewal or surprise recurring billing.

## Operating Context
- Users watch on living room TVs (Firestick, Android TV, Apple TV, Samsung/LG Smart TVs) as well as mobile devices and laptops.
- Configured via industry-standard IPTV player apps (TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, XCIPTV, IBO Player) using Xtream Codes API or M3U playlist credentials.
- Customers evaluate services based on stream stability during live sports events, channel breadth, fast activation delivery, and honest pricing.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Channels & VOD**: 25,000+ live channels worldwide and 120,000+ movies/TV shows with 4K Ultra HD streaming.
- **Connections**: 2 simultaneous streams included on every subscription plan.
- **Free Trial**: 24-hour full access trial with no credit card required ($0).
- **Billing**: 1, 3, 6, and 12-month prepaid tiers ($16, $39, $60, $90). No auto-renewal.
- **Payment Methods**: Cryptocurrency, PayPal, Stripe.
- **Delivery & Support**: Fast 5–15 minute credential delivery via email and WhatsApp.
- **Content Governance**: Strict adherence to first-party product facts in `src/lib/site-config.ts` (`PRODUCT_TRUTHS`); unverified performance superlatives or false guarantees are prohibited.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: TryIPTV (domain: tryiptv.com).
- Voice & Tone: Clear, transparent, direct, technically competent, and grounded in truth. Avoid scammy or deceptive subscription marketing tropes.
- Visual Language: Modern dark interface with vibrant emerald green accents (#22C55E / #16A34A), clean typography (Outfit for headings, Inter for body/UI), subtle borders, soft shadows, and high scanability.

## Evidence on Hand
- Source of truth for product facts: `src/lib/site-config.ts` (`PRODUCT_TRUTHS`).
- Documented quality and claims standards: `docs/content-quality-system.md`.
- Device setup documentation: `src/app/setup/` and `src/app/devices/`.
- Pricing matrix and plan definitions: `src/lib/site-data/pricing.ts` and `src/app/pricing/`.

## Product Principles
1. **Radical Transparency**: Upfront prepaid pricing, clear connection allowances, and unambiguous trial parameters with no hidden fees or auto-charges.
2. **Frictionless Activation**: Swift 5–15 minute onboarding with comprehensive, tested guides for common players and hardware.
3. **Evidence-Backed Reliability**: Honest representation of stream specifications and features without fabricated benchmarks or impossible guarantees.
4. **Multi-Screen Flexibility**: Standard 2-stream support to accommodate simultaneous household viewing across devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Maintain high contrast on dark backgrounds (WCAG AA compliant), clear tap targets for remote and touch control, and readable typography across all viewport sizes.
