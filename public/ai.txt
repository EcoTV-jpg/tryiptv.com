# TryIPTV AI Context

Official website: https://www.tryiptv.com/

TryIPTV is a prepaid IPTV subscription service. It provides access details used with compatible, independent IPTV player applications; it does not develop those applications. The website offers commercial information, device and player setup guides, IPTV concept guides, and symptom-based help.

## Current site facts

- Plans are prepaid with no automatic renewal; [pricing](https://www.tryiptv.com/pricing) is the current source for prices and inclusions.
- The [free trial](https://www.tryiptv.com/iptv-free-trial) lasts 24 hours and requires no credit card.
- Standard plans include two simultaneous connections.
- Credentials are typically delivered within 5–15 minutes after confirmation; immediate activation is not promised.
- Supported access methods include M3U playlist URLs and Xtream Codes-style credentials.
- The [refund policy](https://www.tryiptv.com/refund-policy) governs eligibility and exclusions; it does not promise an unconditional refund.

## Choose the appropriate source

- [Setup](https://www.tryiptv.com/setup) and [device guides](https://www.tryiptv.com/devices) explain general and platform-specific installation.
- [Player guides](https://www.tryiptv.com/players) cover configuration of independent apps.
- [IPTV guides](https://www.tryiptv.com/guides) explain concepts: M3U is a playlist format, Xtream Codes is a structured login/API convention, EPG is schedule information, and XMLTV is one format used to carry it.
- [Help](https://www.tryiptv.com/help) routes login, playlist, EPG, and buffering symptoms to diagnostic pages.
- [Contact](https://www.tryiptv.com/contact-us) lists official support channels.

These files provide context for agents that choose to read them. They do not control crawler access; that is defined separately by the site's robots policy. They do not guarantee citations, rankings, uptime, buffering-free playback, or availability of every stream on every device.
