# Keyword map (on-page SEO, 2026-10-20)

Each page has one primary keyword and three to five secondary keywords, matched to search intent.

- **Sources:** the queries in `seo/keyword-map.csv`, the current titles, H1s and opening copy (crawled from the `technical-seo` build), and the competitor research in `seo/research/on-page-seo.md`.
- **Volumes and difficulty are blank on purpose.** Ahrefs returned "Insufficient plan", and we don't estimate numbers.
- **Validate against real data before relying on this map.** Use Search Console (Performance → Queries per page) and the Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance report once both are set up.

**Intent labels**
- **Commercial:** the visitor is choosing or buying.
- **Informational:** learning or how-to.
- **Navigational:** looking for a specific TryIPTV page.

The primary keyword was checked on all 46 pages. It appears in the first 100 words and in the H1 everywhere, and in the `<title>` everywhere after this branch. Before this branch, `/pricing` was the only exception.

## Cannibalisation: flags and proposals (need your decision)

No URL was changed and no redirect was added. Each item below is a proposal.

| # | Pages | Overlap | Proposal |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | `/devices` vs `/setup` | Both hubs target "IPTV setup guide(s)". `/devices` title: "IPTV Setup Guides for All Compatible Devices". `/setup` title: "IPTV Setup Hub: From Credentials to Your Device". | **Keep both and split the intent** (recommended). `/devices` targets "IPTV compatible devices" (device list). `/setup` targets "how to set up IPTV" (credentials → device → player). This needs a new `/devices` title and H1, which I haven't changed without your OK. A merge (301 `/devices` → `/setup`) isn't advised, because `/devices` is the parent of all 11 device URLs and their breadcrumbs. |
