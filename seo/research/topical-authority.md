# Topical authority research (tryiptv.com)

Compiled 2026-10-20 from five parallel research passes.

- **[Official]** marks a vendor statement. **[3P]** marks third-party data, which is correlational unless stated otherwise.
- The plan built from this research (topic tree, gaps, calendar) is in `seo/topic-map.md`.

**Bottom line**
- Google's "topic authority" system is officially **news-only**. For other sites, the equivalents are the helpful-content "primary purpose or focus" question and site-level quality.
- AI citation evidence:
  - Topic coverage helps a **little**: ρ≈0.25–0.29 on AI Overviews, AI Mode and Perplexity; 0.13 on ChatGPT.
  - **Page count barely matters** (ρ≈0.19).
  - Brand mentions and reach matter more.
  - Pages built for fan-out variants are **scaled content abuse**.
- **So: fewer, stronger pages with answer-first sections, tight internal linking, and no keyword-variant page sets.**

## 3. AI Overviews and AI Mode: query fan-out

| Date | Source | Method | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025-05-20 / 2025-11-18 | [Official] [AI Mode update](https://blog.google/products/search/google-search-ai-mode-update/), [Gemini 3](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/gemini-3-search-ai-mode/) | — | <ul><li>"a multitude of queries simultaneously".</li><li>Deep Search "can issue hundreds of searches".</li><li>Gemini 3: fan-out "getting a major upgrade".</li><li>No official count for standard AI Mode.</li></ul> |
| 2025-11-28 (updated 2026-09-17) | [3P] [Surfer](https://surferseo.com/blog/query-fan-out-impact) | 10k keywords, 33k fan-outs | <ul><li>ρ 0.77 between the number of fan-outs a page ranks for and its AI Overview citations.</li><li>Only about 27% of fan-outs repeat across runs.</li></ul> |
| 2025-11-21 / 2026-01-27 | [3P] [Seer](https://seerinteractive.com/insights/gemini-3-query-fan-outs-research) | Gemini 3 API, 501 prompts; then 1,300 runs | <ul><li>10.7 fan-outs on average (8.52 in the follow-up).</li><li>95–99% have zero search volume.</li><li>Track themes, not exact strings.</li></ul> |
| 2026-03-02 | [3P] [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-citations-top-10/) | 863k SERPs | 37.9% of AI Overview citations are in the top 10, so about 62% come from outside it. |
| 2026-07-21 | [3P, vendor] [MaxAEO](https://maxaeo.ai/blog/ai-mode-content-strategy/) | 19,412 AI Mode citations, 41 hub-vs-spoke domains (B2B) | <ul><li>Hub pages: median 5.4 citations per URL; narrow pages: 1.6.</li><li>Facets covered correlate with the number of direct-answer sections (r=0.61), not word count (0.08).</li><li>Pages split below 3 facets averaged 0.9 citations.</li></ul> |
| 2026-09-19 | [3P, single author] [arXiv 2609.23162](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23162) | 34,960 runs | Being retrieved drives brand mentions: 2.8–3.8% when not retrieved, 91–100% when the own domain is cited and a branded fan-out runs. |

**Gaps:** no study measures how often People Also Ask or autocomplete match real fan-outs. Autocomplete is used here as a demand signal only. A Gemini API grounding run (`webSearchQueries`, 5–10 runs per seed) would give observed fan-outs; it needs an API key.

## 4. AI engines: does depth increase citations?

| Date | Source | Method | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-28 | [3P] [Wellows](https://wellows.com/research/topical-authority-ai-citations-study/) | 247k snapshots, 151 topics, held-out halves | <ul><li>Coverage vs citations: ρ AI Overviews 0.29, AI Mode 0.28, Perplexity 0.25, Gemini 0.20, ChatGPT 0.13.</li><li>Citation reach on other topics predicts better (0.29–0.37).</li><li>The authors say it "does not show what happens when a site publishes more pages."</li></ul> |
| 2026-04-13 | [3P] [AirOps × Indig](https://www.airops.com/report/the-fan-out-effect-what-happens-between-a-query-and-a-citation) | ChatGPT UI, 16,851 queries | <ul><li>A heading matching the query (≥0.90) gets 41.0% citations vs 30.2% (<0.50).</li><li>Covering 26–50% of fan-out subtopics beats 100%.</li><li>500–2,000 words is best; 5,000+ drops.</li></ul> |
| 2025-12-12 | [3P] [Ahrefs, 75k brands](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations/) | Spearman | <ul><li>Number of site pages ρ≈0.19: "almost no relationship between content volume… and AI visibility".</li><li>Brand mentions ≈0.66–0.74.</li></ul> |
| 2025-12-15 | [3P] [SE Ranking AI Mode](https://seranking.com/blog/how-to-optimize-for-ai-mode/) | 2.33M pages, SHAP | <ul><li>Domain traffic and referring domains dominate.</li><li>Sections of 100–150 words do best.</li><li>A length "sweet spot" of about 1,500 words.</li></ul> |
| 2026-08-05 | [3P] [Wellows, domain authority](https://wellows.com/research/does-domain-authority-matter-for-ai-citations/) | 20.5M citations | <ul><li>47.9% of citations go to domains with DA 1–39.</li><li>ChatGPT's median cited DA rose from 35 to 51 in July 2026.</li></ul> |

## 5. Competitors: topics we're missing

Sitemaps were inventoried on 2026-10-20: troypoint.com, firesticktricks.com, bossofiptv.com, primestreamus.com, smartiptvapp.com, sacotv.com, usiptvprovider.com and cordcuttersnews.com.

**High-value, low-risk gaps**
- **Players:** "best IPTV player for [device]" (8 autocomplete variants); IBO Player; Smart IPTV; VLC; Kodi PVR; Flix, IMPlayer and UHF.
- **Devices:** Formuler (MyTVOnline); Nvidia Shield (a section); Hisense VIDAA and Vizio; Android phones; Xbox and PlayStation.
- **Multi-device and connections explainer:** high buying intent.
- **Error codes:** 401, 403, 404, 458, 511, 522 and "code 7".
- **Symptoms:** freezing every 10 seconds; black screen with sound; catch-up not working; not working with a VPN.
- **Comparisons:** vs streaming services, vs satellite, TiviMate vs Smarters.
- **Guides:** recording and DVR; a VPN explainer; how to spot unsafe IPTV apps and sites.

**Covered by competitors but excluded**
- country and US-state pages
- language and diaspora pages
- sports, PPV and event pages
- Downloader codes and MAC "generators"

All of these carry doorway or copyright risk; see `seo/topic-map.md`.
